What the prototype does

Log the cancellation once; it offers the opening to one client at a time until someone says yes

1
Staff log the cancellation
Stylist, service, date and time, in a short form.
2
Find who fits
Wants that service, free at that time, and named that stylist or no one. Earliest to join goes first.
3
Text one person
Never a group text. They have 15 minutes to reply.
4
"Yes" is booked right away
Nobody else can claim it. Staff get a reminder to update Square.
↺  "No," or no reply in 15 minutes? It moves on to the next person automatically.

Late replies are never booked

Someone who answers after their turn is told: "This offer has expired and the opening may have gone to someone else."

Staff can cancel any time

If the original client comes back or the stylist can't make it. Whoever holds the offer is told it's no longer available.

Safe if the computer restarts

It runs on Temporal, a workflow engine that keeps each opening's countdown and replies safe even if the computer restarts.

When something goes wrong, it says so

A phone number doesn't workThat client is skipped and marked "Couldn't text." The next person gets the offer.
Texting is down for a momentIt keeps retrying. If it still can't send, it stops and says "Please contact clients directly."
Too close to the appointmentNo new offers within 45 minutes of the start time. The opening is marked "Too late."
Nobody fits, or everyone passesA notice says nobody is available, so staff know to stop waiting.

What's simulated or not included yet

This is a working prototype to try the rules safely; it does not touch real clients yet

Simulated or left out for now
  • No real texts. Messages appear on a pretend client phone on screen, with Yes and No buttons.
  • No Square or Google Sheets connection. Staff type in the cancellation and update Square themselves.
  • Made-up waitlist. The clients are fictional sample people.
  • No logins and no way to edit the waitlist on the page.
  • "Demo speed" shortens offers to 30 seconds, only for showing it live. Normal is 15 minutes.
Please confirm
  • The 45-minute cutoff. We stop sending offers 45 minutes before the appointment, and an offer never runs past that point. Is that the right amount of time to get ready and arrive?
  • People who say no or don't reply aren't asked again for that opening, but stay on the waitlist for future ones. Is that what you want?

A practical next step

Try it for real with one stylist for 3 weeks, with Square still updated by hand

Before starting
Connect a real texting service to the salon's number. Load that stylist's waitlist.
Weeks 1–3
Every cancellation for that stylist goes through the system. Staff keep updating Square themselves.
End of pilot
Review together: how many openings were filled, any client complaints, and whether 15 minutes and the 45-minute cutoff feel right.

What we need from you to start

  1. Which stylist to start with.
  2. OK to text from the salon's number, and approval of the wording of the offer text.
  3. Confirm or adjust the 15-minute reply window and 45-minute cutoff.

Why this is low-risk

  • Only one stylist's openings are affected.
  • Square stays exactly as it is today.
  • Staff can cancel any offer, and can stop the pilot at any time.
